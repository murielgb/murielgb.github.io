Professional Summary

Evolutionary biologist studying the diversity, domestication, and evolutionary history of the date palm (Phoenix dactylifera) and its wild relatives. My research combines population genomics, archaeogenomics, archaeobotany, seed morphometrics, and ethnobotany to understand how crop diversity emerged and evolved through interactions between humans and plants.
