New reference genome for the date palm: a gap-free, telomere-to-telomere assembly of the variety Ajwa, together with 19 additional high-quality genome assemblies, including the barnī from al-ʿUlā oasis, now published in [_Scientific Data_](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-08193-0) (Celii et al. 2026).
