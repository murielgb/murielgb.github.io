Our study of farmers' knowledge of date palm diversity in Taghit oasis (south-western Algeria), combining folk nomenclature, fruit morphometrics and four decades of varietal turnover, has been accepted for publication. The [preprint](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11025038/v1) is available now, stay tuned for the final version!
